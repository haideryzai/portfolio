about

Backend-first, full-stack when it counts.

I started at CodeFreaks building B2B SaaS products from the ground up — a hiring platform for private equity firms, a UK vehicle recovery marketplace — then moved into ad-tech at Advergic, where I built the full programmatic advertising stack: bidding, fraud detection, identity resolution, an Android SDK, and the dashboards that tie it together.

Most of what I build lives where latency, scale, and correctness all matter at once — request paths measured in milliseconds, pipelines processing real-time signal, systems that have to be right the first time. Lately that's extended to agentic AI: I'm building a copilot that investigates and fixes infrastructure issues on its own, with a human approving the risky parts. Outside client work, I freelance on Upwork as a Top Rated contractor.
